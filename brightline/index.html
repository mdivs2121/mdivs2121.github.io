FAQ

Common questions

Do you charge a trip fee?

No trip charge when we do the repair. If you decide not to go ahead after the diagnosis, there's a flat $79 service call.

How fast can you come out?

Most calls received before noon get a same-day visit. Emergencies like active leaks or no water are prioritized, including evenings and weekends.

Will the price change once you start?

No. We diagnose the problem first and give you a firm price. If we find something else while working, we stop and ask before doing anything extra.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Brightline is fully licensed and insured, and we're happy to send proof before your appointment.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, check, Zelle and all major credit cards, paid when the job is done.
